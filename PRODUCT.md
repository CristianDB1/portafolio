## Stack

## Users

Two audiences, with two clear routes on the page:
- Owners of small and local businesses in Colombia who need a catalog, an ordering flow (WhatsApp), or a management system (gym, inventory, sales).
- Operations/IT decision makers in mid-size companies (logistics, ports, agro, multi-branch) who need traceability, multi-tenant platforms, dashboards, internal tools.

They arrive from a message, a referral or a link in a proposal, and decide in minutes whether this developer can deliver.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and sales page for a freelance full-stack developer (GitHub: CristianDB1) to sell custom software services to companies. Success = a visitor writes via WhatsApp/email to request a quote or a call.

## Positioning

A single developer who ships complete, working products (database, backend, web, deploy), shown by real repositories, not mockups. Spanish-speaking, close, fast to reply.
