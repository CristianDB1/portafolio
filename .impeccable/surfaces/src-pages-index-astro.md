# Surface brief: landing / portfolio (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Visitors: (a) small-business owners, (b) mid-size company operations/IT. Action: write via WhatsApp/email for a quote. Proof: real repositories, real project READMEs, real catalog photos. No AI mention. No invented clients, prices, metrics or demo URLs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a Colombian talonario (carbon-copy remision/cotizacion pad): sheets of thin coloured paper torn off one by one, filled in by hand. Refuses the dark-gradient developer-portfolio template and the card grid of services.

OWN-WORLD: Canary, pink and mint copy-paper sheets plus one white original; single printing ink in ultramarine blue, consecutive numbering and rubber stamps in red; handwriting for the filled-in values; heavy condensed printer's gothic for form titles and field labels; perforated tear edges, ruled lines, checkbox squares. Recognizable with all content removed.

STORY: A visitor ticks "Tengo un negocio" or "Represento una empresa", watches the form fill itself with what that route needs, sees real repos as dispatched items, and signs the form (WhatsApp/email message) to request a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width canary sheet. Printed header band (name, "Software a la medida", red N° 0001). Huge condensed headline "Del talonario al sistema." across the sheet; below it two ruled checkbox rows (negocio / empresa) and handwritten fill-in lines that rewrite themselves per route; primary action is a red-outlined "Firmar y pedir cotizacion" stamp-button bottom right of the sheet. Nav is a thin printed strip above.

FORM: Own-world direction 3 of 7 grounded candidates (container/port stencil, hand-painted shop signs, talonario, banana-box print, chiva bus art, WhatsApp business UI, blueprint). seed: 286d9efb7be6e32b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
